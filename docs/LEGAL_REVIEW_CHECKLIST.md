# Legal and Third-Party Review Checklist

This is an operational checklist, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel.

## Intellectual property and data providers

- [ ] Confirm the repository's code license is intentional and that all included code is owned or properly licensed.
- [ ] Review TCGdex API terms, attribution requirements, card image terms, rate limits, and permitted commercial use.
- [ ] Review TCGplayer and Cardmarket rules for displaying, caching, refreshing, and commercially using their price data.
- [ ] Confirm whether each card image, logo, set symbol, and other artwork may be displayed and cached.
- [ ] Do not imply sponsorship, endorsement, or affiliation with The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures, TCGplayer, Cardmarket, or TCGdex without permission.
- [ ] Add an accurate third-party attribution and trademark disclaimer once the exact obligations are confirmed.

## User-facing agreements and privacy

- [ ] Have counsel review Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, community rules, acceptable-use rules, and support/contact procedures before accounts or analytics are introduced.
- [ ] Document exactly what data is collected, why, where it is stored, retention periods, deletion/export process, subprocessors, and breach response.
- [ ] Do not collect passwords directly; use a reputable authentication provider.
- [ ] Evaluate children's privacy requirements, including COPPA where applicable, because Pokémon games may attract children.
- [ ] Review consumer protection, advertising disclosures, accessibility, and state privacy requirements for target markets.
- [ ] Provide a clear disclaimer that provider quotes may be stale or mismatched and that predictions are for entertainment, not financial advice.

## Payments, prizes, and predictions

- [ ] Obtain explicit payment-provider approval for the exact product and payment flow before charging.
- [ ] Keep demo coins non-transferable, non-redeemable, and without cash value until reviewed.
- [ ] Do not add cash prizes, cash-outs, paid chance-based outcomes, or redeemable tokens without jurisdiction-specific gaming/gambling review.
- [ ] Review refunds, taxes, chargebacks, age restrictions, purchase disclosures, and subscription cancellation requirements if monetization is later approved.

## Current status

Pokediction currently has a code license file in the repository, but this does not establish rights to Pokémon names/artwork or third-party pricing data. No legal approval is implied by this checklist. These items remain open until documented and reviewed.
